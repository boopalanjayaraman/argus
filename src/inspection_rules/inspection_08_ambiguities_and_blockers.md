# Inspection - Ambiguities and Blockers. Always HOLD, do not infer.
1. External max-pressure table: only 2 verified tag examples in inspection_01.
2. Open-ended body material and version lists ("etc.").
3. Regulator torque tolerance not stated. See assembly_04.
4. Type 2412 torque (IV) referenced with no value.
5. Type 3510 procedure states a bonnet torque for Type 3520. See assembly_08.
6. Series 1a bottom flange torque is only embossed on the flange; additional instructions sheet for 2.5-6in is blank.
7. Type 3241 metal bellows sequence ends at placing the body in the vise.
8. All blank torque/leakage table cells.
9. Non-numeric terms: "snug / do not over-tighten", "tighten securely", "acceptable appearance". Require record/photo; no numeric PASS basis.
10. "Comfortably below limit" has no margin %. Replaced by measured < limit.
11. Max inlet pressure P1 and "241 & 251" type mapping must come from the work order.
12. Lubricant names differ by section: black grease, white grease, Never-Seez, 8150-0119, 8150-0111. Check exact lubricant for that type/step.

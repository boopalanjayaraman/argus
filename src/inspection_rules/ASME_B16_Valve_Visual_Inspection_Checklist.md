### Notes
1. Always verify against the edition of ASME B16.34 / B16.5 invoked in the Purchase Order (e.g. 2017, 2020).
2. For **Special Class** valves, refer to ASME B16.34 Annex B for additional surface examination (MT/PT) requirements.
3. The acceptable level for casting surface quality to MSS SP-55 must be agreed in the purchase specification.

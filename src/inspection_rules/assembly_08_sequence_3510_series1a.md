# Assembly - Sequence Type 3510 and Series 1a
Verify assembly record. No record = HOLD. Wrong order/tool/material = FAIL. Source: Sheets 18-20
## Type 3510
Parts verified. Body in vise. White grease seat threads. Counter-clockwise align then clockwise.
Seat torque = 20 Nm for all Type 3510.
Grease gasket area/top. White grease stem at packing.
Bonnet torque: source states 120 Nm "for all Type 3520" inside 3510 procedure = HOLD. Do not auto-apply.
Plug must move smoothly into seat, else FAIL.
2 lock-nuts, stem 1-2mm above top nut, nuts tightened together. Outside 1-2mm = FAIL.
Locking bracket fitted, valve data facing upwards, lock screw tightened.
## Series 1a
Clean machined surfaces with alcohol. PTFE O-ring on seat. PTFE holder bore aligned with inlet. PTFE disc. Elastomer insert.
Bottom flange torque = value embossed on flange. Must be read/recorded, else HOLD.
Anti-rotation pin: stem moves freely up/down, does not rotate, else FAIL.
V-ring packing, packing nut hand-threaded. Retaining cord fully around plug.
Elastomer gasket in bonnet without packing damage. Bonnet bolts cross-wise, push stem periodically.
Mirror check plug/seat alignment. Additional instructions for 2.5-6in are blank = HOLD.

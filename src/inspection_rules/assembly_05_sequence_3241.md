# Assembly - Sequence Type 3241
Verify assembly record. No record = HOLD. Wrong order/tool/material = FAIL.
## Standard - Sheet 14
Parts verified vs work order (material, Cv, size, ANSI); body flange in vise; black grease seat threads; rotate counter-clockwise to align then clockwise; seat torqued with correct tool/multiplier per assembly_02; grease gasket area and top of gasket; white grease plug stem at packing, plug fully into bonnet; bonnet threaded carefully; grease studs, cross-wise bonnet torque; stuffing box snug not over-tightened (record only); stem lock + coupling nuts installed not tightened; remove from vise; test per inspection files.

Source ends at placing body in vise. Completion beyond that = HOLD.
